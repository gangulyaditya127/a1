Incident Correlation Analysis — 4-Dimension Story Map

Every correlation dimension below is grounded directly in the SNOW incident dump (2,717 rows, 111 days). Each dimension answers a different correlation question — and each shows its Top 5 evidence-backed stories in the same animated, annotated format.

⏱ All 4 dimensions enforce strict evidence rules — no dimension infers correlation without a stated, checkable rule

▶ How to Operate This Report

Quick guide — about 30 seconds per story to review.

1
Pick a Dimension cardChoose Horizontal, Vertical, Multi-Day Same-Time, or Recurring Symptom at the top — each answers a different correlation question.
2
Pick a Story tabEach dimension has its own Top-5 stories, ranked by strength of evidence (occurrence count / distinct days).
3
Click ▶ PlayNodes glow and lines draw as each real incident fires in chronological order — with its exact INC number and timestamp.
4
Watch the FeedRight-hand panel lists every incident live — time/date, INC number, application, and description.
5
Use ⏸ Pause / ⟲ ResetInspect any moment, or replay from the start. The Speed slider adjusts playback pace.
6
Read the write-upRoot Cause, Business Impact, Correlation Rule, and a Confidence tag (Confirmed / Likely Real / Needs Validation) are shown under every story.
💡 Tip: For each story, please validate whether the correlation is real, and note any Correlation ID / Problem / Change Request evidence you have — the Confidence tag reflects our data-driven assessment, not proof of causation.
